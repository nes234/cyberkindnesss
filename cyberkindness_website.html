Know the issue

What is cyberbullying?

Cyberbullying is bullying that happens through digital devices and online platforms. It can involve repeated hurtful behavior that makes another person feel targeted, threatened, or humiliated.

💬

Hurtful messages

Sending insulting, threatening, or humiliating messages through chats or social media.

📢

Spreading rumors

Sharing false or harmful stories about someone online.

📸

Sharing without consent

Posting or forwarding someone's private photos, messages, or information without permission.

🚫

Online exclusion

Deliberately leaving someone out of online groups or activities to embarrass or isolate them.

🎭

Impersonation

Pretending to be another person online to damage their reputation or cause trouble.

🔁

Repeated harassment

Continuously targeting a person through comments, posts, or messages.

Why does it matter? Cyberbullying can affect a person's confidence, emotional well-being, friendships, and sense of safety. Online actions can have real-life effects.
Make a positive impact

Small actions. Safer online spaces.

Everyone can help prevent cyberbullying by making thoughtful choices and treating others with respect.

🧠

Think before you post

Pause before commenting or sharing. Ask yourself whether your words are respectful and helpful.

🤝

Show respect

Remember that there is a real person behind every screen. Be considerate, even when you disagree.

🔒

Protect your privacy

Use privacy settings and avoid sharing passwords or personal information with strangers.

📣

Speak up safely

Do not join in or forward harmful content. Support the person being targeted in a safe and respectful way.

🚩

Report and block

Use the platform's reporting and blocking tools when an account or message is abusive.

🧑‍🏫

Ask for help

Talk to a trusted adult, teacher, school counselor, or another appropriate support person.

How CyberKindness spreads awareness

Share learning materials

Create and share educational posters, videos, and information about cyberbullying and its effects.

Promote thoughtful posting

Encourage students to think before posting, commenting, or sharing information online.

Connect people to support

Promote reporting harmful online behavior, blocking abusive accounts, and seeking help from trusted adults or appropriate authorities.

You are not alone

Get help. Take a safe next step.

If you experience or witness cyberbullying, consider saving relevant evidence, using the platform's report and block features, and telling a trusted adult or school support person. If someone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or an appropriate authority.

Remember: You do not have to handle online harassment by yourself.
